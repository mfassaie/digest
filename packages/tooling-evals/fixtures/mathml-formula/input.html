Common Formulas

This page lists two formulas that appear throughout the course, written in MathML so they render natively in the browser.

Quadratic formula

The roots of a quadratic equation are given by:

x = −b ± b2 − 4ac 2a

It is valid whenever the coefficient a is non-zero.

Summation

The sum of the first n natural numbers is:

∑ i=1 n i = n(n+1) 2

This closed form avoids iterating over every term.
