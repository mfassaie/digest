# City approves £40m transit overhaul

Councillors voted on Tuesday to approve a £40m overhaul of the city's ageing tram network, ending a decade of stalled planning. The package funds new rolling stock, signal upgrades, and three extended lines reaching the eastern suburbs.

## What the money buys

Roughly £24m is earmarked for twelve new low-floor trams, with the remainder split between track renewal and a modernised control centre. Officials say the first new vehicles should enter service within eighteen months, subject to supplier lead times.

> This is the biggest single investment in public transport the city has seen in a generation, and it is overdue.

## Reaction

Opposition members welcomed the investment but questioned the funding model, which relies partly on a future increase in parking charges. A residents' group representing the eastern wards called the extended lines "a long-awaited lifeline" for commuters who currently face hour-long bus journeys into the centre.

Construction is expected to begin next spring, with the full programme due for completion by 2030.
